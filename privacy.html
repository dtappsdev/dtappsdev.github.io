Privacy Policy

My HoBeh · Last updated 13 September 2026

My HoBeh is a Singapore 4D and TOTO number checker. The short version: everything you type into the app stays on your phone, and the only company that receives anything about you is Google, for the ads.

What stays on your phone and never leaves it

All of the following is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never uploaded, never sent to us, and we cannot see it:

  • Your saved numbers, and any notes or recorded winnings you attach to them.
  • Photos you attach to a saved number. These are references to photos already on your phone; the app does not copy, upload or transmit them.
  • Your settings: language, 4D/TOTO mode, notification switches, and whether you have accepted the disclaimer.

There are no accounts and no login. There is no server that holds your data, because there is no server.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. It is not backed up to any cloud, which also means it cannot be recovered if you uninstall — the app's Backup & Transfer feature moves it directly between two phones by QR code, with nothing passing through the internet.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of My HoBeh shows ads supplied by Google AdMob.

To select and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier Android and iOS provide for this purpose;
  • general information about your device and the app, such as device model, operating system version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • whether an ad was shown, and whether it was tapped.

This information is collected by Google, not by us. We never see it, and it is never linked to your saved numbers, your notes or your photos — the ad SDK has no access to them.

How Google uses it is described in Google's Privacy & Terms and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices:

  • Buy "Remove Ads" in Settings. This is a one-off purchase that removes every ad surface in the app. With no ads, the ad SDK requests nothing.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form the first time it runs, and your answer decides whether ads are personalised. You can change that answer later from the same form.

Lottery results

The app carries the full draw archive inside it, so checking a number works with no internet connection at all. To pick up draws made since the app was last updated it fetches one public file of published lottery results over HTTPS.

That request is anonymous. It sends no identifier, no account, no saved numbers and nothing about you — it asks for a file and nothing else. Your numbers are never sent anywhere to be checked; the checking happens on your phone.

Notifications

Draw alerts and win alerts are created by the app on your own phone, from the archive already on it. No notification service is involved and nothing about what you are alerted to leaves the device.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and tells the app only whether you own the product. We never see your payment details. Google's handling of a purchase is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Send Feedback in Settings, the app sends the message you typed, plus your platform (Android or iOS) and the app version, to a private messaging channel we read. Nothing else is attached — no identifier, no email address, no saved numbers. If you type your own contact details into the message, we will of course see them, because you sent them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to scan the QR code shown on your old phone during Backup & Transfer. No photo or video is recorded or stored.
  • Notifications — only to show draw and win alerts, and only after you turn a switch on.
  • Photos — only when you choose a photo to attach to a saved number.

The ad SDK also declares network and advertising-ID permissions that are not prompted for and are used only for the ads described above.

Children

My HoBeh is not intended for children. It displays results of legal lottery draws and is designed for adults. Ads are requested with a G content rating and without interest-based targeting of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does with information changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top changes. The ads section above was added on 13 September 2026, ahead of the app update that introduces the ads it describes — before then, the app showed no ads and collected nothing.
