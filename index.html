My HoBeh

A Singapore 4D and TOTO number checker, built to be readable.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Check a 4D or TOTO number against forty years of published draws — 5,600+ 4D draws back to 1986, and every TOTO draw since 2008.
  • Works with no internet connection. The whole archive ships inside the app, so nothing is sent anywhere to be checked.
  • Understands how the bets are actually placed: 4D Roll (R) and iBet arrangements, not just four literal digits.
  • Save your numbers, with notes and a photo, and get a notification on the evening a draw you are watching is published.
  • English and Simplified Chinese. Large type and large touch targets throughout — it is designed for older eyes and hands.

What it is not

My HoBeh displays published lottery results. It does not take bets, sell tickets, pay out money, or simulate gambling. Results come from Singapore Pools' own published figures.

Your data

Your saved numbers, notes and photos stay on your phone. There are no accounts and no server holding them.

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising ID. The full details are in the privacy policy.
